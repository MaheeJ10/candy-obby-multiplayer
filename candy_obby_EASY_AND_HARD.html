Gumdrop Gauntlet
How to play

Explore 100 levels across ten candy worlds, with a finale in each world and 300 optional candy stars. Reach the lollipop flag to clear each level.

Controls

Arrow keys / WASD: move and jump · Space: jump · R: retry · Esc: pause

On a phone, use the arrow buttons to move and the jump button to leap. On a controller, use the stick or D-pad to move and the bottom face button to jump. Start / Options pauses. Change keyboard and controller bindings in Game settings.

Controller: connect it, then press a button to wake it up.

Difficulty

Easy mode gives you an invisible halfway checkpoint in every level and longer-lasting disappearing platforms. Hard mode has no halfway checkpoints and requires tighter timing.

Hard mode: no halfway checkpoints and the original disappearing-platform timing.

Solo play & saving

Standard game is untimed and does not save run results or permanent progress. Timed game saves progress, medals, stars and records. Best-time ghost adds a replay of your fastest recorded solo run. Easy and Hard keep separate time records.

Play without a timer. Run results and progress are not saved.

Play at your own pace, or choose a timed game to earn medals and save records.

Platforms & power-ups

Large spring pads launch you to higher ledges. Snowflake ice builds speed: run across it, then jump near the edge to cross the long gap. Press the opposite direction to brake.

Ride moving platforms and triple spinners, then time your leap. Cross disappearing platforms before they fade. Purple pads with inward arrows shrink under your feet: jump off quickly! They regrow after you leave, and shrink more slowly in Easy mode. In co-op, each player's pads react to their own footsteps. Moon Mints make jumps floatier; Double Pops give you a second jump in midair. With either power, releasing movement stops horizontal drift. Powers reset between levels.

Later worlds add updrafts, rising chocolate, swinging rides, stretchy bubblegum, timed gates, conveyors and wind. Watch each obstacle before committing to a jump.

Hazards

Some levels include gravity-flip sections. Step on a cyan floor button to land on the ceiling above you. Jump away from the ceiling to cross the gaps, then step on a gold ceiling button to restore normal gravity. Later worlds have longer routes, narrower ceiling platforms and larger gaps. Easy gives you wider landings and smaller gaps.

Fudge crushers drop when you approach: wait for the slam, then pass as they rise. Blasters fire regularly. Land on top of their round projectiles to pop them; avoid their sides. Rolling jawbreakers can also be stomped.

Practice, stars & friends

Pip’s Candy Academy teaches the basics with interactive lessons. Candy Workshop lets you practise unlocked levels, slow hazards and set safe practice checkpoints. Practice does not earn campaign rewards.

Collect stars during timed adventures to unlock Candy Cabinet cosmetics. Level & World Stats stores your records. Saves belong to this browser and site.

Online play has six candy sprites, with one player per sprite. Create or join a room, then play together. Players can collide, stand on each other and push nearby teammates. Use the in-game level selector or room vote to change levels.
